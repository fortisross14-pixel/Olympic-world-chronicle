# V2 Final Expansion

This release replaces the prototype's generic post-1896 universe with a persistent historical simulation.

## Major changes from V1

- Complete edition-specific medal-event programmes from 1896 onward.
- Curated Paris 2024 and Los Angeles 2028 programmes.
- Programme additions/removals view.
- Procedural athletes from global country-specific naming pools.
- Persistent athlete careers across Olympiads.
- Sport-dependent debut, peak and retirement ages.
- Fixed rarity/potential with changing edition rating.
- Returning Olympians, retirements and archived stars.
- Country investment, facilities, youth systems and sport priorities.
- Permanent medal archive and country Olympic histories.
- WR/OR lineages with record lifespan.
- More realistic event-specific performance generation.
- IndexedDB V2 saves.
- Reproducible full-history smoke test.
