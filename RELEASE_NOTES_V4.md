# V4 — Complete Athlete Pathways & Olympic Magazine

## Complete Olympic results

- Every scheduled round now generates and stores full athlete standings.
- Results include rank, mark, advancement, elimination and final medal.
- Added daily round-results cards and complete event tables.
- Athlete profiles now connect qualification performance to every Olympic round.
- World and Olympic records can occur in preliminary rounds.
- Medal cards display event records even when they occurred before the final.

## Qualification redesign

- Added sport-specific rankings for the entire active athlete pathway.
- Qualified and failed athletes appear together.
- Added qualification index, world rank, discipline, mark and status.
- Named qualifying competitions now expose browsable standings.
- Each athlete preserves competition rank and global rank in their career history.
- Fixed sport-filter country totals.

## Olympic Magazine

- Added World & Investment, Athlete Life, Pre-Games and Post-Games sections.
- Newcomer and retirement reporting for Epic, Legend and Generational athletes.
- Pre-Games key-event detection for fields with multiple elite athletes.
- Final-Games candidates and elite qualification failures.
- Post-Games multiple medalists, surprise champions and disappointments.

## Country events

- Every cycle now creates 102 developments:
  - 54 mild;
  - 30 significant;
  - 12 big;
  - 6 huge.
- Only the six huge events reach the magazine front page.
- Severity scales investment, facilities, youth, delegation and sporting-priority effects.

## Procedural host alignment

- Procedural hosts now reshape delegation distribution.
- Historical host-country delegation bias is dampened when another city wins.
- Host nations receive a meaningful home delegation and development boost.
- Host election, theme, programme choices and decaying legacy remain connected.

## Flags and rarity

- Replaced visible Unicode flag emoji with image-backed flags.
- Added automatic NOC-code fallback for unsupported or blocked flags.
- Generational rarity is consistently red across lists, badges and profiles.

## Archive and persistence

- Added V4 IndexedDB save slot.
- Current Olympic rounds remain fully detailed.
- Completed-edition round results use compact columnar rows.
- Historical qualifier standings retain useful leaders without duplicating the complete current ranking database.
- Qualified athletes are no longer serialized twice in the active career pool.
